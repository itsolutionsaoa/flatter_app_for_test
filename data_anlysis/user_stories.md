# User Stories

These stories are aligned with `business_requirements_doc.md` v1.1 and cover BR-001 through BR-025.

## Applicant, Student, and Parent Stories

* **US-001:** As an applicant, I want to submit an online application with required documents so that the admissions team can review my eligibility.
* **US-002:** As an applicant, I want to track my application status so that I know whether I am pending review, approved, waitlisted, or rejected.
* **US-003:** As a student, I want to see my daily class schedule so that I can prepare for my lessons.
* **US-004:** As a student, I want to view attendance, grades, report cards, and transcripts once approved.
* **US-005:** As a student, I want to download course materials, submit assignments, and participate in class discussions.
* **US-006:** As a student, I want to request services such as transport, library, documents, or accommodation.
* **US-007:** As a parent, I want to view my child's attendance, grades, schedule, assignments, and evaluation reports.
* **US-008:** As a parent, I want to receive absence, lateness, fee, grade, transport, and emergency alerts by SMS, email, push, or in-app notification.
* **US-009:** As a parent, I want to view invoices, installments, receipts, payment history, and outstanding balances.
* **US-010:** As a parent, I want to pay fees online using supported methods such as card, Mada, bank transfer, or payment gateway.

## Registrar and Academic Stories

* **US-011:** As an admissions officer, I want to configure admission requirements by campus, stage, grade, or program.
* **US-012:** As an admissions officer, I want to review applications, verify documents, update status, and generate admission letters.
* **US-013:** As a registrar, I want to convert approved applicants into active students without re-entering data.
* **US-014:** As a registrar, I want to manage transfers, withdrawals, graduation, reactivation, and student status history.
* **US-015:** As an academic administrator, I want to manage academic years, terms, stages, grades, sections, courses, curriculum plans, and calendars.
* **US-016:** As an academic administrator, I want to create class and exam timetables while checking conflicts for teachers, rooms, sections, and students.
* **US-017:** As an academic administrator, I want to configure promotion rules and publish academic calendars.

## Teacher and Learning Stories

* **US-018:** As a teacher, I want to record student attendance quickly on web or mobile.
* **US-019:** As a teacher, I want to upload learning materials and assignment instructions.
* **US-020:** As a teacher, I want to grade assignments, exams, and assessments using configured grading rules.
* **US-021:** As a teacher, I want to publish approved results and comments to students and parents.
* **US-022:** As a teacher, I want to track student learning progress and participation.
* **US-023:** As a teacher, I want to see my timetable, assigned courses, workload, and tasks.

## Finance and Accounting Stories

* **US-024:** As an accountant, I want to define fee structures, discounts, scholarships, penalties, and optional service fees.
* **US-025:** As an accountant, I want to generate VAT-compliant invoices and installment schedules for students.
* **US-026:** As an accountant, I want to process payments, allocate receipts, issue refunds, and track outstanding balances.
* **US-027:** As an accountant, I want automated journal entries from invoices, receipts, payroll, purchases, inventory, and assets.
* **US-028:** As an accountant, I want to manage chart of accounts, vouchers, banks, safes, cost centers, and financial periods.
* **US-029:** As an accountant, I want trial balance, ledger, account statement, VAT/tax, profit and loss, and balance sheet reports.

## HR and Payroll Stories

* **US-030:** As an HR officer, I want to maintain employee and teacher profiles with personal, administrative, financial, and document data.
* **US-031:** As an HR officer, I want to manage contracts, attendance, departure, overtime, leave, and leave balances.
* **US-032:** As an HR officer, I want to process payroll with earnings, deductions, allowances, benefits, and end-of-service calculations.
* **US-033:** As an HR officer, I want to assign tasks and track staff productivity and performance evaluations.
* **US-034:** As an employee, I want self-service access to payslips, leave requests, assigned tasks, and profile information.

## Procurement, Inventory, and Asset Stories

* **US-035:** As a procurement officer, I want to manage suppliers, purchase requests, quotations, purchase orders, goods receipts, and purchase invoices.
* **US-036:** As a procurement officer, I want to compare supplier quotations and route purchase approvals.
* **US-037:** As an inventory manager, I want to manage items, units, batches, expiry dates, warehouses, stock balances, and stock movements.
* **US-038:** As an inventory manager, I want to transfer stock between warehouses and perform stocktaking with variance approval.
* **US-039:** As an asset manager, I want to register assets, track locations, assignments, maintenance, movement, disposal, and depreciation.

## Operations Stories

* **US-040:** As a transport manager, I want to manage routes, stops, vehicles, drivers, schedules, and student route assignments.
* **US-041:** As a transport manager, I want to track transport fees, vehicle documents, maintenance, and incidents.
* **US-042:** As a library manager, I want to catalog books, manage copies, memberships, issue/return, reservations, penalties, and overdue items.
* **US-043:** As a hostel manager, I want to manage buildings, rooms, beds, requests, allocations, transfers, occupancy, and accommodation fees.
* **US-044:** As a document controller, I want to archive applications, student files, employee files, contracts, invoices, official letters, and expiring documents.

## Communication, Reporting, and Administration Stories

* **US-045:** As a communication manager, I want to broadcast announcements to selected campuses, grades, sections, roles, or individuals.
* **US-046:** As a communication manager, I want multilingual templates and delivery tracking for SMS, email, push, and in-app notifications.
* **US-047:** As an executive, I want dashboards for enrollment, attendance, student performance, fee collection, receivables, HR, inventory, and institutional KPIs.
* **US-048:** As an admin, I want to configure users, roles, permissions, workflows, numbering series, forms, templates, localization, and multi-campus settings.
* **US-049:** As an admin, I want audit logs for sensitive actions such as grade changes, fee changes, payments, journal postings, document access, and permission changes.
* **US-050:** As an integration administrator, I want to configure payment, SMS, email, e-learning, government, tax/e-invoicing, identity, and BI integrations with retry tracking.
